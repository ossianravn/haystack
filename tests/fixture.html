A short guide to booking your next journey.

Cancellation

You can cancel a booking up to 48 hours before departure for a full refund. Later cancellations incur a 25 euro fee.

Luggage

Passengers may bring two bags onboard. Bicycles require a separate reservation.

Refunds

Refunds for cancelled trips are sent to your original payment method within five working days.

Catering

The dining car serves breakfast from seven until ten. Vegetarian meals are available.
